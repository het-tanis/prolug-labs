# See encapsulation in a capture

This lab is part of a larger free workshop and presentation put on by ProLUG.org.
This can be completed on its own, but more context is provided in the study material
found in the ProLUG Discord.

A packet capture is the ground truth for the OSI model. You will capture ICMP
and HTTP on loopback, then label the layers you can actually see.

Evidence goes in `/root/cap`.
