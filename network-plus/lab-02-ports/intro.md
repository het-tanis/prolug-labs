# Ports, sockets and services

This lab is part of a larger free workshop and presentation put on by ProLUG.org.
This can be completed on its own, but more context is provided in the study material
found in the ProLUG Discord.

A port is not open because a firewall says so; it is open because a process is
bound to it. In this lab you inventory listeners, bind one service to loopback,
and prove the difference.

Evidence goes in `/root/ports`.
