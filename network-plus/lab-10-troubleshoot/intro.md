# Break it, find it, fix it

This lab is part of a larger free workshop and presentation put on by ProLUG.org.
This can be completed on its own, but more context is provided in the study material
found in the ProLUG Discord.

Three faults were planted: a blackhole route, a wrong hosts entry, and added
latency. Work them in order, using evidence rather than guesswork.

Target: `10.99.0.1` on `dummy0`; name: `app.lab`.
